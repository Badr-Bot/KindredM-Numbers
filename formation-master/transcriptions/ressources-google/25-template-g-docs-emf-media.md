‼️ Checklist

Website

* Homepage & Collection Pages: Optimized banners and clear offer messaging above the fold.

* PDPs (Product Detail Pages): Add urgency signals (stock counters, delivery cut-offs, reviews, BFCM-only USPs).

* Cart & Checkout: Simplify steps, reduce friction, enable express checkout.

* Technical Prep: Stress-test site load speed, set up backups, check mobile UX.

Email 

* Warm-up Sequences: Send teaser campaigns 2–3 weeks before BFCM.

* VIP Early Access: Segment top customers for early entry to offers.

* Flash Sale Alerts: Automated flows tied to limited-time drops.

* Post-Purchase: Upsell & retention emails for new BFCM customers.

Creative

* Main Offer Creative: Hero visuals, bold discount framing, urgency copy.

* Flash Offer Assets: Adapted banners for site, email, and social.

* Angles to Test: Scarcity (“Don’t Miss Out”), Exclusivity (“VIP Access”), Value (“Best Price of the Year”).

* Content Format Mix: Short-form UGC, static banners, GIFs, and high-conversion video ads.

Paid Search

* Ensure branded keywords + “BFCM” variations are live.

* Set custom sitelinks & ad copy for offers.

* Increase budgets on high-intent search days.





Paid Social

* Scale budget with teasing campaigns in early November.

* Prepare all campaigns by J-5

* Launch Top View Campaigns on TT for 21/22

* Launch Top Feed Campaigns for 22-30

* Retarget engaged users with urgency creatives.

* Use dynamic product ads for cross-selling.





Other

Inventory: Secure stock levels for high-demand SKUs.

Customer Support: Prep team for higher volume & FAQ automation.
