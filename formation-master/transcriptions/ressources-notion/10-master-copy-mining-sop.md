RULES
Focus on visual proof, not explanations
One idea = one image (no carousels)
Headlines must be under 7 words
Avoid hype words and medical claims
Make the contrast obvious (before / after, wrong vs right, old vs new)
Prioritize clarity over cleverness
Assume the ad is watched with no sound
