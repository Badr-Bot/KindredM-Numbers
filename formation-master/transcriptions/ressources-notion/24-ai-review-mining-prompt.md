## Contenu du document

How to conduct AI-assisted social listening
Téléchargez un fichier CSV contenant vos avis clients (Amazon, etc.).
Importez ce fichier dans un outil d'IA de votre choix, adapté à cette tâche (Chat GPT, Octane AI ou Reviews AI).
Utilisez le prompt ci après pour 
Vous devez formuler votre requête de manière à ce qu'une fois l'analyse des avis terminée, l'IA vous fournisse un rapport détaillé sur votre produit et vos clients. L'objectif est que l'IA vous fournisse un rapport sur les points clés concernant le produit et les clients.
Product
Value propositions of the product
Unique features and benefits of your product
Customer
Pain points
Desired outcomes
What led to the customer making the purchase
Misconceptions
Failed solutions
Objections
Once you’ve got this report you can start picking out key insights and inferences from the data it gives back to you. 
PROMPT ​
Here’s a prompt we use for the brands that we work with that you can use to aid this process
Once you have provided the data from the CSV you can use this prompt to help the AI tool breakdown the data for you
“Now, I will present a collection of reviews for [BRAND]'s [PRODUCT] available at [PRODUCT URL]. Please take a moment to read through them carefully, and once you're done, I will proceed with a series of questions based on these reviews. 
Here are the reviews:

[REVIEWS]

Let's analyze the reviews to identify the most common customer perspectives and insights related to the product. We will focus on the following categories, each accompanied by an appropriate emoji for clarity:

Product:
Main Unique Value propositions (What are the main reasons why people purchased this product? (benefits/advantages))
Unique Features/Benefits (What are the unique selling points this product has over its’ competitors?)
Customer:

Customer Pain Points - What are the most significant or frequently mentioned challenges or issues that customers sought to address or alleviate using this product?

Customer Desired Outcomes - What are the most commonly expressed goals or desired results that customers expected or hoped to achieve when purchasing this product?

Customer Purchase Prompts - What specific events or triggers led customers to consider purchasing this product? What influenced their decision to explore or invest in it?

Customer Misconceptions - What misconceptions or misunderstandings did customers have about the product or brand before their experience with it? What false beliefs or assumptions have they come to realize are not true?

Customer Failed Solutions - What alternative solutions or approaches have customers previously attempted but found ineffective or unsatisfactory in addressing their needs? What previous methods or products have failed to meet their expectations?

Customer Objections - Why did customers initially doubt or question whether this product would work for them? What concerns or reservations did they have? What specific aspects or perceptions about the product caused hesitation or apprehension before making a purchase?

Please use Markdown formatting to ensure the content is presented in an organised and visually appealing manner.”
You can then use this information to create angles, creatives, and messaging to directly address what people are identifying here. 
(For this to work effectively, it’s important you provide chat GPT with at least 50 of both positive and negative reviews.)
PROMPT  2
Now Based on these reviews, please organize your insights using the EPIC Framework, which classifies marketing angles into four psychological quadrants:
 Framework EPIC
E – Emotional: Angles that trigger emotions, feelings, or empathy (love, comfort, fear, pride, nostalgia, joy).
P – Practical: Angles that highlight solutions, functionality, or measurable benefits (results, relief, time-saving, comfort).
I – Identity: Angles that target self-image, belonging, and transformation (status, confidence, community).
C – Critical: Angles that trigger logical reasoning (proof, value, comparison, savings, evidence).
 Please identify and summarize:
Top Emotional Angles (E)
→ What emotional desires or frustrations drive customers?
→ Example output: “Finally feeling confident again in my clothes”, “A product that makes me proud to wear it.”
Top Practical Angles (P)
→ What concrete problems or functional improvements do they highlight?
→ Example output: “No more sweating”, “Softer fabric that lasts longer.”
Top Identity Angles (I)
→ What transformation or social perception does the customer aspire to?
→ Example output: “The boxer for real men”, “Finally look like a high performer.”
Top Critical Angles (C)
→ What rational or comparative justifications are used to buy or trust the product?
→ Example output: “Better quality than Burberry at half the price”, “Scientifically proven materials.”
Then, for each category, provide:
Top 3 recurring customer phrases or expressions (verbatim style)
Emotional subtext (what emotion it triggers)
Angle summary (1 sentence) ready to use in ad copy
Example hook (one-liner that could start an ad)
