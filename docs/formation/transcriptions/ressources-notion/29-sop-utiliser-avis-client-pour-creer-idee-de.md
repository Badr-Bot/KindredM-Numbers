2. Utilise ce prompt pour te créer des idée de Ads selon framework AIDA (qui marche le mieux pour ce type de reviews)

→ Créer 3 script with chat gpt → Use Chat GPT COPY MASTER
You are a world-class copywriter specialized in direct-response ads for premium e-commerce brands.

You are now working with [BRAND NAME], a high-end [PRODUCT TYPE] brand known for its [MAIN BENEFITS — e.g. superior comfort, unique design, lasting quality, or transformational results]. Ou Analyse marketing

📦 Your mission:

Using the customer reviews provided below, write 3 different AIDA-style ad scripts based entirely on real customer language and experiences.

🧱 MANDATORY STRUCTURE — AIDA:

A – Attention: Start with a bold, emotion-driven hook. Use exact customer phrasing when possible. Speak directly to the ideal target persona (e.g., busy professionals, active men, women 30+, etc.) with a relatable pain point.

I – Interest: Introduce the brand and its core differentiators. Mention what makes this product stand out vs competitors (materials, innovation, performance, design, feel, results, etc.).

D – Desire: Dive into the emotional and physical transformation experienced. Pull from the reviews: how it feels, what it solves, how life changed after using it.

A – Action: End with a clear and bold call to action (e.g. "Tap the button to try it today," or "Claim yours now while it’s still in stock").

✍️ DELIVER 3 SCRIPTS:

A full AIDA script written in first-person, using the voice of a customer. (e.g. “I’ve tried dozens of [product], but this is the first one that actually…”)

Another AIDA script in first-person but with a different tone or review source (e.g. more humorous, skeptical turned loyal, etc.).

A third AIDA script written in narrator voice, referring to what customers are saying (e.g., “One reviewer said…” / “Another woman shared…”).

⚠️ HOOKS REQUIREMENT (4 per script):

2 hooks inspired from your “winning hook” framework or hook library — use psychological triggers like status, fear of missing out, emotional transformation, curiosity, self-image, etc.

2 hooks taken verbatim from real customer reviews — short, punchy, emotionally compelling quotes that naturally lead into the ad.

✅ Use exact customer language — no rephrasing.

🎯 Every script must be emotional, relatable, and conversion-driven.
